# SuryaCart 🌱

### Solar-Powered Cold-Storage Cart Concept for Street Vendors

**SuryaCart** is an innovation project exploring how solar energy and insulated storage could help street vendors keep perishable produce fresh for longer.

## 🎯 Problem
Street vendors may face challenges such as hot weather, food spoilage, and limited access to affordable cold storage.

## 💡 Proposed Solution
SuryaCart explores a mobile cart concept combining:
- ☀️ Solar panels for renewable energy
- 🔋 Battery storage for energy when needed
- ❄️ Insulated storage with a suitable cooling system
- 🌡️ Temperature monitoring

## 🌍 Expected Benefits
- Potentially reduce food waste
- Support street vendors
- Explore cleaner energy solutions
- Improve produce storage

## 🧪 Project Status
This is a proposed concept. Its actual performance, technical feasibility, and cost need to be verified through testing and supplier quotations.

SuryaCart is a proposed mobile cart concept that combines solar energy and an insulated storage compartment to help street vendors keep fruits and vegetables fresher for longer.

Our mission

Explore an affordable, practical cart concept for small vendors who need to protect perishable produce during the working day.

01 • Understand the need

The problem we want to solve

🍅

Perishable goods

Heat can speed up the deterioration of many fruits and vegetables.

💸

Possible income loss

Produce that becomes unsellable can reduce a vendor's earnings.

🔌

Power access

A mobile vendor may not have convenient access to a reliable electricity supply.

Our design question

Can a solar-assisted, insulated mobile cart offer a practical way to help street vendors protect perishable produce?

These are problem statements to investigate, not claims that a survey or experiment has already proved them.

Design principle

The solar panel may charge a battery or power suitable equipment through a properly designed electrical system. The insulation reduces heat transfer. Actual cooling performance depends on the equipment, weather, and available energy.
